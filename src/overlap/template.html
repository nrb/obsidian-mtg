Colors: letters from W U B R G for the card’s color identity as it counts for fetch-land needs and the color charts (one letter is mono, two or more is multicolor), C for colorless, L for a land. Physical cards use the highest copy count of each card across the decks in the set, since a card can move between decks. Main deck and sideboard are combined. Basic lands are left out of the totals and listed separately, because a deck can run more than four. Strategies counts distinct playstyles (Grixis and Esper are both Reanimator). Broad types counts Aggro, Midrange and Combo. Best balance keeps only the sets that no other set beats on variety and card count together. Shared copies counts the copies you can reuse between the decks (total copies minus physical cards needed), with its share of all copies in brackets. Least overlap puts the sets that share the fewest copies first. Total with basics adds the basic lands, counting each basic at the most any one deck plays, since only one deck is built at a time. Smallest total ranks by that figure. Decks at once is the most decks from the set that you could build together from the pool plus the extra cards you enter above, so it starts at 1 when the extra cards are 0. Extra to build all is how many cards you would add to build every deck in the set at the same time. Built at once picks how many decks of the set you want to have built at the same time (1 by default, which is the plain total above). For 2 or more, two more columns give the physical cards needed to build any that many decks together: each card is counted at the sum of its highest copy counts across that many decks, and Smallest pool and Smallest total then rank by those figures. Ties break on fewer unique names.

Counter types

A counter type is listed when a card in the deck (main or sideboard) places it, uses it (loyalty on planeswalkers), or has it as part of its own text, per each card's oracle text. Keyword actions that make counters count (amass, fabricate, undying, adapt). Cards with no counters are omitted. Hover a mark for the card names.

Packages

Cards

PackageDecksCardsCopies

A package is a set of cards that are always in the same decks: every selected deck that plays one card of the package (main deck or sideboard) plays all of them, and no other card is in exactly those decks. That keeps each package as small as the lists allow; a card that also shows up in one more deck lands in a different package. Only packages of at least two different cards in two or more selected decks are listed, and basic lands are left out. Copies after a card are per deck, in the order of the Decks column; SB marks sideboard copies. The Copies column is the most copies of the package any one deck plays.

Interchangeable cards

FunctionCards in the selected decksPool nowStandardizedStandardize on

Each row is a family of Scryfall oracle tags you picked as doing the same job. Copies after a card are per deck (main deck plus sideboard). Pool now counts each card at the most copies any one deck plays. Standardized is the pool if every deck used the same card for that job (self-mill may use two cards, ideally at 1 and 2 mana; Overlord of the Balemurk counts as 2 for its impending cost), at the most copies of the job any one deck plays; above 4 per card you would still need another card. Standardize on is the black/green-castable card your selected decks already play the most (for self-mill, the most-played at mana value 1 and at 2). Cards tagged mana-value-matters, such as Culling Ritual, only hit cheap or specific mana values, so they get their own row instead of counting as a replacement for unrestricted removal like Assassin's Trophy. Lessons are left out because they travel with the Eyetwitch package, except Origin of Metalbending, which costs the same as other artifact and enchantment removal.

Off-color cards

CardNeedsDecksSuggested black/green substitutesYour suggestions

A card is listed when casting it needs white, blue or red mana that black or green cannot pay (hybrid costs with a black or green half, such as Fulminator Mage, count as black or green), or when it is a land that makes white, blue or red. Colors a deck keeps on purpose are not counted: Zombardment keeps red for Goblin Bombardment. Suggested substitutes are Modern-legal cards castable with black, green or colorless mana that share the card's Scryfall oracle tags (from Scryfall's Oracle Tags data, art tags not used; cosmetic tags such as cycles or punny names are skipped; shown in grey), ranked by how specific the shared tags are, with cards already in your decks (★) first. They are a starting point from tag overlap, not tested swaps, and were looked up on 6 Nov 2026. For off-color lands the substitute is a Swamp or a black/green land once the deck no longer needs that color. Your suggestions are saved with the plugin and included in the JSON export.

Only fetch lands that can find a Swamp are swapped (Polluted Delta, Bloodstained Mire, Verdant Catacombs, Marsh Flats), since every deck here is black. Each deck keeps its number of fetch lands in the main deck and its limit of four copies per name, sideboard copies included. A deck keeps at least as many fetch lands for a color it needs as it plays now, so a deck that needs blue keeps its Polluted Deltas. Fetch lands for colors a deck does not need can be swapped for any Swamp fetch the pool already has. The pool then holds only as many fetch lands as the most demanding deck needs. This works on one deck built at a time and leaves every other card alone.
